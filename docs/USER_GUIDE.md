# Staff User Manual: LMO Digital Records Management System (DRMS)

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Target Audience:** Records Officers, Records Managers, Office Managers, and Authorized Staff  

---

## 1. Signing In & Installing the Mobile App (PWA)

1. Open the LMO DRMS portal in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.
2. Sign in using your official email address (`@lmo.go.ug`) and password.
3. **Mobile Installation:** On Android or iOS, tap **Install App** in the top bar (or *Add to Home Screen* in browser menu) to install LMO DRMS as a standalone icon on your phone.

---

## 2. How to Scan & Register an Incoming Document

1. Click **Scan / Register** (`+ Register / Scan Record`) in the navigation bar.
2. **Enter Official Reference & Metadata:**
   - **Official Reference Number:** Type the sender's exact reference number as printed on the letter (e.g., `MKCCMA/PS/2026/084`). Leave blank if unnumbered.
   - If a similar reference number already exists, a yellow **Potential Duplicate Warning** will appear automatically.
   - Enter the **Subject / Title**, **Originating Organization**, **Sender**, **Recipient**, **Date Issued**, and **Date Received**.
3. **Classify the Document:**
   - Select the **Category** (e.g., *Official Invitation*, *Inter-Ministerial Correspondence*, *Legal Notice*).
   - If registering an **Invitation**, enter the **Event Date & Time** so it appears on the Invitations schedule.
   - Select the **Confidentiality Classification** (`Public`, `Internal`, `Confidential`, or `Strictly Confidential`).
4. **Attach Files or Scan with Smartphone Camera:**
   - **Smartphone Camera:** Tap **Open Live Scanner** or **Capture Photo Page** to photograph physical pages one by one.
   - **Desktop Scanner / Computer Upload:** Drag and drop PDF, JPEG, or PNG files into the upload zone.
5. Click **Complete Registration & Run OCR**. The system assigns a unique internal tracking ID (e.g., `LMO-2026-00001`), stores the files in private storage, and extracts searchable OCR text.

---

## 3. Searching & Retrieving Records

1. Use the **Global Search Bar** at the top of any screen or open **Document Register**.
2. Type any word from the document title, sender, reference number, or a phrase inside the scanned letter body (e.g., `Nakivubo Channel`).
3. Narrow results by **Category**, **Document Type** (e.g., *Invitation*), **Date Range**, **Office**, or **Confidentiality**.
4. Matching keywords inside OCR-extracted text are highlighted in yellow (`<mark>`).

---

## 4. Archiving & Restoring Records (Records Managers)

1. Open a closed document and click **Archive Record**.
2. Enter the mandatory **Archival Reason** and confirm.
3. The record moves to the **Digital Archive Vault** (`/archives`) while keeping 100% of its files, OCR text, and audit trail intact.
4. To reopen an archived document, open it from the Vault, click **Restore to Active Register**, and record the restoration reason.
